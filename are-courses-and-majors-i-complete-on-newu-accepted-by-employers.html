Are Courses and Majors I Complete on NewU Accepted by Employers?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Not automatically. No employer is required to accept a NewU certificate, and NewU is not accredited, so it is not a degree and not transfer credit. But for an employer hiring on demonstrated ability, the case is strong — because a NewU certificate is proof of real, graded work that anyone can check in seconds, for free. See Do Employers Accept NewU Credentials? for what an employer actually sees.

Why employers should accept them: the work is real

Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass, and the coursework is real work — not a multiple-choice quiz. For a course in a major it is a case analysis; for a stand-alone DIY course it is work tailored to the topic. The work is graded by AI with consistency checks, and anything flagged goes to a human reviewer — no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision. And 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. A Certificate of Completion proves one course of that work; a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education proves 40.

Why employers should accept them: the proof is checkable

An employer does not have to take anyone’s word for it. They can verify the certificate themselves in seconds, for free, at any time — no phone calls, no transcript requests. And unlike a traditional transcript, which shows a course title and a letter grade, the record behind a NewU certificate shows the graded work itself. An employer hiring for what a person can actually do has more to check here, not less.

Why employers should accept them: the record cannot be rewritten

Your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it — no changed grades, no fudged passes, nothing. The anchor is a permanent public record that does not depend on NewU staying in business.

Why employers should accept them: the work is verifiably yours

Your verification photograph is shown to the employer on the verification page, next to your name and the VALID verdict — so the employer knows the person presenting the certificate is the person who did the work. And you control what they see: you share your grades and certificates only with people you choose, by email, and you can take that access away at any time.

The honest limit, stated plainly

NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. If an employer, a licensing body, or a program requires a degree from an accredited university, a NewU certificate is not that. A college will not accept it as transfer credit. The credential is for employers who want to check real work, not for roles that require an accredited degree by rule.

What to tell an employer

Share the verification link from your credential page. They can check it themselves in seconds, for free, with no account — and see the courses, the graded work, and the photograph that ties the work to you. If they want to know more, the verification page and the certificate speak for themselves.

Frequently asked questions

Does NewU guarantee employers will accept my certificate?
No. Whether a given employer accepts it is their call. What NewU guarantees is that the proof is checkable.

Can an employer check my certificate without an account?
Yes. With a valid verification link, no account is needed.

What if the job requires an accredited degree?
Then a NewU certificate is not a substitute. It is proof of work, not a degree.

Does a single-course certificate carry the same weight as the major?
It proves one course’s work; the major proves 40. Both are the same kind of proof — graded work, verifiable in seconds — at different scales.

The bottom line

Not automatic — but the proof is stronger than a claim, and any employer can check it for themselves.
