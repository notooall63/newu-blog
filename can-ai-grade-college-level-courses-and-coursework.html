Can AI Grade College-Level Courses and Coursework?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. On NewU Institute, AI generates the coursework and AI grades it. The coursework is generated to match the course content, and it is graded by AI — with random sample submissions re-graded several times to check the grader stays within its acceptable range. If it drifts outside that range, the Admin adjusts that coursework’s grading rubric.

How the grading stays honest

Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass. On top of that, 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. If your explanation comes up short, it goes to an actual human reviewer, and no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision. So the AI does the grading, but a human is always the final check when something is questioned.

What the AI does — and what it doesn’t

The AI generates coursework relative to the course content and grades your completion of that work. It does not decide your record on its own: your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it — no changed grades, no fudged passes, nothing. And your work is never used to train AI: grading, tutoring, and coursework generation run on Cloudflare Workers AI, which does not keep your content after the request is done and does not use it to train models.
