Can I Create My Own College Course?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. On NewU Institute, you can create your own college course — the platform calls it a DIY Course. You learn the subject anywhere you like, then prove what you learned on NewU through coursework and AI grading. And a DIY Course can count toward a major.

How a DIY Course works

You bring your own learning. That can be a book, a video, a free online course, or your own study — wherever the subject is taught well. NewU does not make you log into another platform or buy course materials. You learn where you learn best, then you come to NewU to prove it.

Proving it means submitting coursework on the topic — a written case analysis, with citations to the sources you used — and having it graded by NewU’s AI. You need 70 out of 100 to pass, the same bar as every other NewU course. Random finished courses also get a spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words, and a human reviewer is the backstop.

What it can count toward

A DIY Course is not just a one-off exercise. It can count as one of the 40 courses in a major — core, general education, or elective — as long as the topic fits that part of the major and the course meets NewU’s post-secondary standard. There is no cap: all 40 courses in a major can be DIY Courses if you want them to be. You can also take a DIY Course on its own, without enrolling in a major, and earn a Certificate of Completion for that single course.

What it is not

A DIY Course is not a shortcut, and it is not a way to skip the work. You still do the coursework, and you still have to pass it at the same 70-out-of-100 bar. The difference is only where the learning happens: you choose the source, and NewU checks the result. And it is still not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education for a full major, or a Certificate of Completion for a single course.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use a particular textbook or platform to learn?
No. You can learn from a book, a video, a free online course, or your own study. NewU never requires you to log into another platform for free materials.

Does a DIY Course cost extra?
No. If you are enrolled in a major, a DIY Course inside that major is part of your plan — $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once. A stand-alone DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

You can create your own college course on NewU Institute. Learn anywhere, prove it through graded coursework, and count it toward a major — or earn a certificate for that one course alone.
