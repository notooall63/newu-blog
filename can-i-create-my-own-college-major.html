Can I Create My Own College Major?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. On NewU Institute, you can create a major you design yourself — the platform calls it a DIY major. It is one of the two ways in: explore 100 curated major structures across nine fields, or create a DIY major around your own goals.

How a DIY major works

You design the major around your own goals, instead of picking a pre-built structure. It is built from the same free online courses — from community colleges to Ivy League institutions and everything in between — and the platform creates coursework relative to those courses’ content, grades your completion of that work, and keeps track of your progress with blockchain authentication. When you finish, you receive the same Certificate of Post-Secondary Education, which any employer may freely verify at any time.

What stays the same

A DIY major is held to the same standard as a curated one. Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass, and 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. If your explanation comes up short, it goes to an actual human reviewer, and no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision. Your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it.

What it is not

A DIY major is not a shortcut, and it is not a different credential. It is the same Certificate of Post-Secondary Education, earned the same way — the only difference is that you chose the shape of the major. And it is still not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to design a major, or can I pick one?
Either. You can explore the 100 curated major structures, or create a DIY major around your own goals.

Does a DIY major cost more?
No. The platform costs the same either way: $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once. All course materials are free online things, and you will not incur tuition debt.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

If none of the 100 curated majors fits what you want to learn, you don’t have to settle — you can design your own major around your own goals, and earn the same verified Certificate for it.
