Can I Enroll in More Than One Major?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. You can enroll in any major at any time, and in more than one major. The only limit is that one certificate can only count toward one major.

How enrolling works

Enrollment is never gated: you can enroll in any major at any time, whether it is one of the 100 catalog majors or a DIY major you designed yourself. There is no application, no admission decision, and no waiting period. You can be enrolled in several majors at once and work through them side by side, or one after another, at your own pace — there is no deadline on any of them. Your plan covers your graded work across all of them: $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once.

The one limit: one certificate, one major

A single course certificate can only count toward one major. If you earn a Certificate of Completion for one DIY Course, you can roll it into one major’s course type — and if it fits more than one course type, you choose once, and that choice is permanent. The certificate stays valid on its own either way, and it still takes $25 off the $999 one-time fee ($12.49 if it was enhanced), off the one-time fee only. But it cannot count toward two majors at once. If you want the same topic to count in a second major, you do the coursework for it there.

Frequently asked questions

Does a second major cost extra?
No. Your plan covers graded work across every major you are enrolled in. The price is $79.99 a month or $999 once, not per major.

Can I switch majors?
Yes. Enroll in any major at any time. Progress you earned in one major stays yours — it is anchored to Bitcoin and cannot be lost.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Enroll in as many majors as you like, whenever you like. Just remember: one certificate counts toward one major.
