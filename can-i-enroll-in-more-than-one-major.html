Can I Enroll in More Than One Major?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. You can enroll in more than one major. Each major requires its own payment: $79.99 a month or $999 once per major. The only limit is that one certificate can only count toward one major.

How enrolling works

You pay for a major before you enroll in it. There is no application, no admission decision, and no waiting period — but enrollment is not free. You pay $79.99 a month or $999 once for that major, and then you are enrolled in it. You can be enrolled in several majors at once and work through them side by side, or one after another, at your own pace — there is no deadline on any of them. Each major has its own payment.

The one limit: one certificate, one major

A single course certificate can only count toward one major. If you earn a Certificate of Completion for one DIY Course, you can roll it into one major’s course type — and if it fits more than one course type, you choose once, and that choice is permanent. The certificate stays valid on its own either way, and it still takes $25 off the $999 one-time fee ($12.49 if it was enhanced), off the one-time fee only. But it cannot count toward two majors at once. If you want the same topic to count in a second major, you do the coursework for it there.

Frequently asked questions

Does a second major cost extra?
Yes. Each major requires its own payment: $79.99 a month or $999 once for that major. A payment for one major cannot be transferred to another major.

Can I switch majors?
There is no switching majors once a payment has been applied to one. A payment for one major cannot be transferred to another major. To take up another major, you pay again for that major: $79.99 a month or $999 once. Progress you earned in one major stays yours — it is anchored to Bitcoin and cannot be lost.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Enroll in as many majors as you like — each one has its own payment. Just remember: one certificate counts toward one major.
