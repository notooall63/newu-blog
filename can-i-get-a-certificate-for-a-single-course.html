Can I Get a Certificate for a Single Course?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. You do not have to enroll in a major to earn a certificate on NewU Institute. A Certificate of Completion costs $19.99 and certifies a single DIY Course — one subject you learned and proved through graded coursework.

How it works

You pick a topic and learn it anywhere — a book, a video, a free online course, your own study. Then you submit coursework on that topic to NewU and it is graded by AI. You need 70 out of 100 to pass, the same bar as every NewU course. When you pass, you receive a Certificate of Completion carrying the name of the course you completed.

You pay the $19.99 before submitting the coursework, so the certificate is issued as soon as you pass. There is no major to enroll in first, and no monthly plan required.

What the certificate is — and is not

A Certificate of Completion is a real, verifiable credential for that one course. Like every NewU credential, it is anchored to the blockchain and carries a QR code and an employer verification link, so anyone can check it in seconds, for free. It is distinct from the Certificate of Post-Secondary Education, which is the credential for completing a full 40-course major — the two are different certificates, and this post’s companion, What is the difference between a Certificate of Completion and a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education?, sets them side by side.

It is not a degree, and NewU Institute is not an accredited university. A Certificate of Completion certifies one course, honestly and verifiably — nothing more, nothing less.

What you can do with it later

A Certificate of Completion can also roll over into a major, if the topic fits one of that major’s course types. It stays valid on its own either way, and rolling it over takes $25 off the $999 one-time major fee — or $12.49 if the course needed AI enhancement to meet the post-secondary standard. The discount applies to the one-time fee only, never to the $79.99-a-month price. See Can I use a single-course certificate toward a major? for the full picture.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be enrolled in anything first?
No. A stand-alone DIY Course and its Certificate of Completion need no major enrollment and no monthly plan.

Can I earn more than one?
Yes. Each certificate is for one course, and you can earn as many as you like — each one can roll into a major later, one course under one major each.

The bottom line

One course, one certificate, $19.99 — no major required, and verifiable by anyone, any time.
