Can I Get a Degree Without Going to College?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

No — not a degree. NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. But you can earn a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education without going to college: complete a major built from free online courses, with graded coursework and a blockchain-verified record, and you receive a Certificate any employer can verify for free, at any time.

What you can get instead

The path is straightforward, and it runs at your pace: choose a major (100 curated structures across nine fields, or a DIY major around your own goals), learn from free online courses at your own pace, complete AI-generated coursework and get it graded, and build a verified record one step at a time — pausable indefinitely, as needed. When you finish, you receive a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education.

For the fuller picture, see Is there an alternative to a traditional college education? and What is the difference between a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education and a traditional degree?

What it costs

All course materials are free online things, and you will not incur tuition debt. The platform costs $79.99 a month — which you can pause at any time and pick up right where you left off — or $999 once.

How it is checked

Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass, and 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. If your explanation comes up short, it goes to an actual human reviewer, and no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision. Your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it. Any employer can check your Certificate themselves in seconds, for free, at any time.

The bottom line

You cannot get a degree without going to college — but you can get a real, checkable Certificate of Post-Secondary Education without going to college, and without tuition debt.
