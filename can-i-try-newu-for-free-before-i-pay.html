Can I Try NewU for Free Before I Pay?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. You can browse the catalog of 100 majors and the free course content without paying anything. You only pay when you want graded coursework and certificates.

What is free

All of the course materials are free online things — from community colleges to Ivy League institutions and everything in between — and you can look through them before you commit to anything. You can explore the 100 majors, see how each one is structured across Major Core, General Education, and Free Electives, and read the free content of any course. Creating an account and completing your profile costs nothing.

What needs a plan

Graded coursework and certificates need a plan: $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once for lifetime access. That is what pays for the coursework, the AI grading, the progress tracking, the blockchain verification, and the certificate. A single DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99, paid before you submit the coursework. And it is still not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education for a full major, or a Certificate of Completion for a single course.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a card to browse for free?
No. Browsing the catalog and the free course content needs no payment of any kind.

Can I start a course for free and pay later?
Yes. You can look through the free materials first, and pay only when you are ready to submit graded coursework.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Look around for free, as long as you like. Pay only when you want your work graded and certified.
