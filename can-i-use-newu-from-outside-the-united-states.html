Can I Use NewU from Outside the United States?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. Anyone, anywhere, online. There is no geographic restriction, and employer verification works worldwide.

How it works from anywhere

NewU is entirely online: the courses are free online courses, the coursework is submitted on the platform, the grading is done by AI, and the certificates are anchored to Bitcoin. Nothing about it depends on where you live. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code, and you learn from wherever you are. The same goes for verification: an employer anywhere in the world can open the verification link you issue and see your credential, your name, your photograph, and the VALID verdict — for free, in seconds.

What is the same everywhere

The price is the same: $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once, and a single DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99. The standard is the same: coursework graded by AI at 70 out of 100 to pass. And the honest limit is the same everywhere too: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree, so its certificate is not transfer credit at an accredited college in any country. What it is, everywhere, is proof of completed, graded work that any employer can verify.
