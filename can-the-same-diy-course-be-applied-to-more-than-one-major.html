Can the Same DIY Course Be Applied to More Than One Major?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

No. One certificate counts as one course under one major. If your topic fits more than one major — or more than one part of a major — you choose where it counts when you roll it over, and that choice is permanent.

Why only once

A major is 40 distinct courses. Counting the same certificate twice would mean counting one course as two — so each certificate is used once.

The choice is yours

When you roll a certificate in, you pick the major and the course type it fits. That choice is permanent, so pick the major where it helps you most. See Can I use a single-course certificate toward a major? for how rollover works.

What stays yours

The certificate itself stays valid on its own, verifiable by any employer, no matter which major it counts toward — or whether it counts toward any major at all.

More than one major

You can enroll in more than one major at a time — but each certificate counts toward only one of them. A second major needs its own 40 courses. See Can I enroll in more than one major?.

Frequently asked questions

I enrolled in two majors — does my certificate count in both?
No. One certificate counts as one course under one major.

Can I change my mind after choosing?
No. The choice is permanent, so choose the major where the certificate helps you most.

Can I earn the same topic again for another major?
The same certificate can never be used twice. A second major needs its own course for that slot.

The bottom line

One certificate, one course, one major — and the choice sticks.
