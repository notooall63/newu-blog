Do DIY Courses Include Languages?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. A language is a topic like any other for a stand-alone DIY course. Learn it anywhere — on Duolingo, Babbel, Rosetta Stone, Busuu, Pimsleur, Jumpspeak, in a class, with a tutor, or on your own — then prove it on NewU and earn a Certificate of Completion for $19.99.

How it works

You propose the topic — for example, “Spanish — Level 1” — and NewU’s AI creates an assignment tailored to it. For a language, that means written questions and answers in the language, at the level you claimed. There is no speaking test and no audio: the level is established through written questions and responses in the language itself. The coursework is graded by AI, and you need 70 out of 100 to pass. The certificate names the topic you proposed.

What the certificate shows — and what it does not

The certificate shows the topic and that you passed the graded coursework for it. It does not carry a proficiency rating — there is no proficiency scale on NewU, so do not read a level into it beyond the topic name. See Does My Certificate Show My Skill Level?

What about a major?

A language certificate can count toward a major, but only at rollover, and only if the topic fits one of the major’s course types and meets the post-secondary standard. Practising a language does not count as substance on its own. See Can a DIY Course on a Language or an Art Skill Count Toward a Major? and How Can I Know If a Completed DIY Course Will Be Applicable to a Major?

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Duolingo?
Yes. Duolingo, Babbel, Rosetta Stone, Busuu, Pimsleur, Jumpspeak, or any app or course you choose — learn it anywhere, prove it on NewU.

Do I have to write in the language?
Yes. The questions and answers are in the language you are learning, at the level you claimed.

Is there a speaking test?
Not yet. The level is established through written questions and responses.

Can I pick any language?
Yes. Any topic can earn a Certificate of Completion.

Does it cost extra?
No. A language certificate costs the same $19.99 as any stand-alone Certificate of Completion.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Yes — languages are in. Learn it anywhere, prove it on NewU, in writing, in the language.
