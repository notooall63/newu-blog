Do I Get a Transcript?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. Your transcript is a verified, printable record of your courses and progress — a transcript page plus a downloadable PDF with a QR code — free to view and download forever.

What is on the transcript

Your transcript lists the courses you have completed in your major, with what you studied, when, and how it was graded. Every completed course on it is anchored to Bitcoin, so the record is verifiable — not just a list you typed up yourself. When you finish all 40 courses in a major, your Certificate of Post-Secondary Education is issued together with the transcript, once all of your course credentials are anchored. A single DIY Course earns its own Certificate of Completion instead, and that certificate is verifiable the same way.

What it costs — and what it does not do

Nothing. Viewing and downloading your transcript is always free, and cancelled or paused users keep read access to their records forever — you can still view and download your transcript and your certificates even if you are not on a plan. What the transcript is not: it is not a degree transcript from an accredited university, and it does not carry transfer credit. NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What it is, is a verified record of completed, graded work that any employer can check.

Frequently asked questions

Can an employer check my transcript?
Yes. You issue a verification link to an employer’s email, and anyone with the link can view the credential — the transcript’s QR code links to the same verification record.

Is my transcript updated as I go?
Yes. It reflects your courses and progress as you complete them, and it is issued together with your certificate when you finish the major.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Yes, you get a transcript — verified, printable, downloadable as a PDF with a QR code, and free to view and download forever.
