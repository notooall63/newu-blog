Do I Have to Finish the Whole Free Course Before I Can Do the Coursework?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

For a listed course, yes — it counts as “watched” at its full expected course hours, and then you do the coursework. For a DIY Course, there is no hours rule at all: you learn the topic anywhere, then prove it through coursework and AI grading.

How it works for listed courses

A listed course is a free online course that NewU has placed under one of your major’s course types. To count it, you work through the course in full — it counts as watched once you have put in its full expected hours. That is the whole point of a listed course: the hours are the learning, and the coursework is the proof. The coursework itself is a 1,000–1,500-word case analysis with citations, graded by AI at 70 out of 100 to pass.

How it works for DIY Courses

A DIY Course has no hours rule. You learn the topic anywhere you like — a book, a video, a free online course, your own study — and you prove what you learned through coursework and AI grading. There is no clock to run and no watch time to log. The proof is the coursework, not the hours. All 40 courses in a major can be DIY Courses if you want them to be.

Frequently asked questions

Can I skip ahead to the coursework on a listed course?
No. The listed course counts as watched at its full expected hours, and the coursework follows the learning.

What if I already know the subject?
Use a DIY Course. Learn anywhere, prove it on NewU through coursework and AI grading, and count it toward your major if it meets NewU’s post-secondary standard.

The bottom line

Listed courses: finish the full expected hours, then do the coursework. DIY Courses: no hours rule — learn anywhere, prove it through coursework.
