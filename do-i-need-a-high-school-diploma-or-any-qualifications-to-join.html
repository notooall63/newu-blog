Do I Need a High School Diploma or Any Qualifications to Join?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

No. There is no entrance test, no diploma requirement, and no admission gate. Anyone 18 or older can enroll in any major at any time.

What NewU does ask for

NewU asks for an account and a profile: your email address, your legal name, date of birth, address, phone number, emergency contact, and highest education, plus a verification photograph of yourself. Only two things must be real: your email address and your photograph. Nothing is verified against any ID or database, and no government ID is required. Your photograph is how future verifiers confirm the work is yours — it is shown to an employer on the verification page, not on your certificate.

What NewU does not ask for

NewU does not ask for a diploma, a transcript, test scores, or any prior qualification. There is no application to be accepted or rejected, and no one reviews your background before you start. You pick a major — or design your own — and you start learning. The bar is in the work itself: coursework is graded by AI at 70 out of 100 to pass, the same bar for everyone. And it is still not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education for a full major, or a Certificate of Completion for a single course.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an age limit?
NewU is for users 18 and older. There is no upper age limit.

Do I need to have finished high school?
No. There is no diploma requirement of any kind.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — create your account and explore the 100 majors. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

No diploma, no test, no admission gate. If you are 18 or older, you can enroll — and the only bar that matters is the work you do.
