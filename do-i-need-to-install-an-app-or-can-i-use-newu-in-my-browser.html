Do I Need to Install an App, or Can I Use NewU in My Browser?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

No install required — NewU works in your browser. There is an “Install as App” button if you want it on your home screen, but it is entirely optional.

How it works in the browser

NewU is a web app: you open newu.institute in any modern browser, sign in with your email address and a one-time code, and everything is there — the catalog, your courses, your coursework, your progress, and your certificates. There is nothing to download, nothing to update, and nothing that only works on one kind of device. Your progress is tied to your account, not to a device, so you can switch between a phone, a tablet, and a computer freely.

What “Install as App” does

If you want NewU on your home screen like an app, the “Install as App” button in the header does that. It installs the same web app — not a separate app from a store — so it updates itself whenever the site updates, and there is no separate version to keep track of. On a phone, tapping it opens your browser’s install prompt; on a browser without one, it shows you how to add it to your home screen instead. If you never tap it, nothing changes: the browser version is the full version.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a NewU app in the app stores?
No. The web app is the app version — there is no separate app to download from a store.

Does installing it cost anything?
No. Installing is free, and it does not change your plan: $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — open it in your browser and explore the 100 majors. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Use NewU in your browser — no install needed. The “Install as App” button is there if you want it on your home screen, and it is the same app either way.
