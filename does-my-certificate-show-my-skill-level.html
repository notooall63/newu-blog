Does My Certificate Show My Skill Level?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

No. Your certificate names the topic you proposed and shows you passed the graded coursework for it, at 70 out of 100. It does not carry a proficiency rating, a skill grade, or a level.

For a language: no proficiency scale

There is no proficiency scale on NewU. If your topic was “Spanish — Level 1,” the “Level 1” is the level you claimed when you proposed the topic, and the questions and answers were at that level — but the certificate itself does not add a separate proficiency rating. Do not claim a level NewU does not test. See Do DIY Courses Include Languages?

For an art or a skill: no ranking

The certificate shows the topic and the pass. It does not grade the artwork on a scale or rank your skill. See Do DIY Courses Include Art-Based Courses?

What an employer sees

The verification shows the topic, the graded work behind it, and that you passed — and the record is anchored to the blockchain, so an employer can check it themselves in seconds, for free, at any time. The certificate proves you did the work and passed the coursework tailored to the topic.

Frequently asked questions

Can I say I am “certified” in Spanish?
You can say you earned a Certificate of Completion for the topic — but do not claim a proficiency level NewU does not test.

Will employers understand what the certificate means?
The verification page shows the topic, the graded work, and that it passed. An employer hiring on demonstrated ability can check it in seconds.
