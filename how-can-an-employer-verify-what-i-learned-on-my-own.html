How Can an Employer Verify What I Learned on My Own?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Give them your verification link. On NewU Institute, self-taught learning becomes a Certificate of Completion, and that certificate comes with a link you issue to an employer’s email address. The employer opens it and sees the credential, your name, your photograph, and a VALID verdict — backed by a Bitcoin anchor. No phone call, no transcript request, no account needed.

How the verification works

It starts with a DIY Course. You learn the topic anywhere, then prove it on NewU through coursework and AI grading — you need 70 out of 100 to pass. When you pass, the result is anchored to Bitcoin, the public blockchain, and you receive a Certificate of Completion ($19.99 for a single course). That anchor is permanent: it cannot be edited, deleted, or lost, and it does not depend on NewU staying in business.

Then you issue a verification grant. You enter the employer’s email address, and NewU sends them a link. Anyone with the link can open it and view the credential. The link stays valid until you revoke it. If your account is ever suspended, your grants stop working until your account is restored — they are never revoked by suspension, only frozen.

What the employer sees

The verification page shows the certificate itself, your name, your photograph, and a VALID verdict — plus the Bitcoin proof behind it. Your photograph is the key to trust here: it is how the employer confirms the work belongs to the person in front of them. It is never printed on the certificate itself; it only appears on the verification page, next to your name and the verdict. The employer can also download the Bitcoin proof and check the anchor independently, without NewU’s involvement.

Why it holds up

Most self-taught learning is unverifiable: an employer has only your word. A NewU certificate changes that, because three separate things back it up. First, the work was graded — coursework with citations, 70 out of 100 to pass, with spot checks and a human reviewer as the backstop. Second, the result is anchored to Bitcoin, a public record no one controls. Third, your photograph ties the record to you. An employer does not have to trust you or NewU. They can check it themselves, for free, in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Does the employer need an account?
No. Anyone with the verification link can open it and view the credential — no account, no login, no phone call.

Can the link expire?
No. A verification grant stays valid until you revoke it. It only stops working while your account is suspended, and it works again when your account is restored.

What if I cancel my plan?
Your certificates stay valid and verifiable. Cancelled or paused users keep read access to their records forever, and the Bitcoin anchor means the record cannot be lost.

The bottom line

An employer verifies what you learned on your own the same way they verify anything else on NewU: they open your verification link and see the credential, your name, your photograph, and a VALID verdict, backed by Bitcoin. Proof, not a claim.
