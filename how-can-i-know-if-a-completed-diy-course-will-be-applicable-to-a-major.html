How Can I Know If a Completed DIY Course Will Be Applicable to a Major?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Preview it on the certificate page before enrolling. The preview runs the same rollover assessment and shows which majors the certificate applies to and whether the discount is full or partial — without enrolling you in anything. That preview is the answer; the rollover assessment is the final word.

How the binding answer works

When you preview a certificate, or when you ask to roll it into a major, NewU’s AI looks at the certificate record itself — the topic, and whether the course met the post-secondary standard when it was earned — and gives one of three answers: it applies as-is, it applies after an enhancement, or it does not apply. That verdict is the final word. See Can I use a single-course certificate toward a major? for the full picture.

The admin’s general answer

Email newuinstitute@aol.com with your certificate topic and the major you are asking about. An admin can check the topic against the major’s course types and give a general answer — including whether the major is even a possible fit for it — without you running the rollover first. The admin’s answer is general, not binding: the rollover assessment is still the final word.

If you built the course inside the major

If you created the DIY course inside the major, relevance was already checked when you proposed the topic — it fits by design, and there is nothing to ask about.

What if it does not apply

The certificate stays valid on its own, verifiable by any employer, whether or not it ever counts toward a major. A stand-alone certificate is worth earning on its own terms.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn’t NewU just tell me upfront?
NewU now does — use the preview on the certificate page. It shows which majors the certificate applies to and whether the discount is full or partial, before you enroll or roll anything over. The preview runs the same assessment, and the assessment is the final word.

Does the admin’s answer guarantee the rollover?
No. It is a general answer, not a binding one. The rollover assessment is the final word.

Can I ask about more than one major?
Yes. The preview shows every major the certificate can be applied to, each with its full or partial discount. You can also ask an admin about each major you are considering, and the admin can give a general answer for each one.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Use the preview on the certificate page to see where it applies — and either way, the certificate stays yours.
