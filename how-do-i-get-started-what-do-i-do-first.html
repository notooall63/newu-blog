How Do I Get Started? What Do I Do First?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Create an account, complete your profile, pick a major (or design your own), enroll, and start a course. That is the whole path. You do not pay anything until you want graded coursework.

Step by step

1. Create an account. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code sent to that email. There is no password to forget. You also verify a recovery email during setup, in case you ever lose access to your main one.

2. Complete your profile. You fill in your legal name, date of birth, address, phone number, emergency contact, and highest education, and you provide a verification photograph of yourself. Only two things must be real: your email address and your photograph. Your photograph is how future verifiers confirm the work is yours — it is shown to an employer on the verification page, not on your certificate. No government ID is required.

3. Pick a major — or design your own. Browse the 100 majors, each 40 courses across Major Core, General Education, and Free Electives. Or design a DIY major of your own, 40 courses built around your goals. You can enroll in any major at any time.

4. Enroll and start a course. Pick a course, work through the free materials, and do the coursework when you are ready. You can start with a listed course or a DIY Course on a topic you choose yourself.

When do I pay?

Payment is needed for graded work: $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once for lifetime access. Until then, you can browse the catalog and the free course content for free. A single DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99, paid before you submit the coursework. And it is still not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education for a full major, or a Certificate of Completion for a single course.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any qualifications to join?
No. There is no entrance test, no diploma requirement, and no admission gate. Anyone 13 or older can enroll — under 18s need a parent or guardian's permission.

Can I change my major later?
Yes. You can enroll in any major at any time, and you can enroll in more than one major. One certificate counts toward only one major.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — create your account and explore the 100 majors. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Account, profile, major, enroll, start a course. Five steps, no admission gate, and no payment until you want your work graded.
