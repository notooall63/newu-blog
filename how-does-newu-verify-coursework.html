How Does NewU Verify Coursework?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

In three layers: the coursework is graded, the grading is checked, and the record is anchored to the blockchain. You complete AI-generated coursework matched to the course content and get it graded; random sample submissions are re-graded several times to check the grader stays within its acceptable range; and your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it. The result is a Certificate any employer can verify for free, in seconds, at any time.

Layer 1 — the grading

The coursework is generated to match the course content, and it is graded by AI. Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass.

Layer 2 — checking the grader

Random sample submissions are re-graded several times to check the grader stays within its acceptable range. If it drifts outside that range, the Admin adjusts that coursework’s grading rubric. On top of that, 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. If your explanation comes up short, it goes to an actual human reviewer, and no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision.

Layer 3 — the blockchain record

Your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it — no changed grades, no fudged passes, nothing. And your photo has one job: it is only ever used to verify that the work is yours.

What an employer sees

An employer can check your Certificate themselves in seconds, for free, at any time — and see that you did the work, you put in the time, you know the materials. You share your grades and Certificates only with people you choose, by email, and you can take that access away at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI keep or learn from my work?
No. Grading runs on Cloudflare Workers AI, which does not keep your content after the request is done and does not use it to train models.

What if the AI grades me wrong?
1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check, and if your explanation comes up short it goes to an actual human reviewer. No action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision.
