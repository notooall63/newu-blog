How Long Does Blockchain Anchoring Take?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Usually within a few hours, and up to 24 hours. Your certificate is valid from the moment it is issued — anchoring is the permanent public proof that follows, not what makes the certificate valid.

What you see while you wait

While anchoring is in progress, your credential page says “Anchoring to Bitcoin — usually within a few hours, up to 24. Your credential is safe and you don’t need to do anything.” Once it finishes, the page shows “Anchored to Bitcoin,” with the block number and the transaction. There is nothing for you to do while you wait.

Why it takes time

NewU anchors credentials to Bitcoin every hour. Your credential joins the next hourly run after it is issued, so a credential issued just after a run waits for the next one.

What anchoring does not change

Anchoring adds the permanent public proof. It never changes the grade, the issue date, or whether the certificate counts. And a major’s Certificate of Post-Secondary Education is issued once the major is finished and every credential in it is anchored — so anchoring is the last step before the major certificate arrives. See What happens to my certificate if NewU ever closes? for what the anchor is and why it is permanent.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download proof of the anchor?
Yes — your credential page has a timestamp proof download.

What if it has been more than 24 hours?
Email newuinstitute@aol.com with your credential name and issue date.

Does the anchor identify me?
No. Only hashes are anchored, never your photograph or personal details.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

A few hours, up to a day — and nothing for you to do while you wait.
