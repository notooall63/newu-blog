How Long Does It Take to Finish a Major? Is There a Deadline?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

There is no deadline. NewU is self-paced — you finish a major as fast or as slowly as your life allows, and you can pause indefinitely. How long it takes depends on the major and your pace.

What sets the pace

Every major is 40 courses. Each listed course takes its full expected hours to count as watched, so a major with longer courses takes longer than one with shorter ones — and your own pace decides the rest. Some learners work through a course a week; others do one a month around a job and a family. Both are fine. There is no semester, no term, and no date by which you must finish. If life gets in the way, you pause — and on the monthly plan you can pause the payment too, then pick up right where you left off whenever you are ready.

What does not expire

Your progress does not expire. Every course you complete is anchored to Bitcoin, so your record is permanent and cannot be lost — not by a pause, not by a cancelled plan, not by time passing. Your certificates stay valid and verifiable. Cancelled or paused users keep read access to their records forever, so nothing you finished is ever taken away.

Frequently asked questions

Can I finish faster by doing DIY Courses?
Yes. A DIY Course has no hours rule — you learn the topic anywhere, then prove it through coursework and AI grading. All 40 courses in a major can be DIY Courses if you want them to be.

Does pausing cost me anything?
No. The monthly plan can be paused at any time, and your progress and certificates are untouched while you are away.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

There is no deadline on NewU. A major takes as long as it takes — and your progress is anchored permanently, so it waits for you.
