How Many DIY Courses Can Be Applied to a Major?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

All 40. There is no cap on DIY courses in a major — every single course can be one.

The two ways in

Build the course inside the major, or earn a stand-alone Certificate of Completion and roll it in. Either way, one certificate counts as one course. See Can I use a single-course certificate toward a major? for how rollover works.

The structure still applies

The 40 courses still have to fit the major’s structure — the right mix of core, general education, and elective courses. DIY changes how you prove each course, not what the major requires.

What it costs

Rolling in a stand-alone certificate takes $25 off the $999 one-time major fee — $12.49 if the course was enhanced. A DIY course completed inside a major counts toward the 40 but does not change the price. The discount applies to the one-time fee only, never to the $79.99-a-month price.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do an entire major as DIY courses?
Yes. All 40 courses can be DIY, in any combination with listed courses.

Does a DIY course take longer than a listed course?
There is no deadline either way. A listed course counts as watched at its full expected hours, while a DIY course has no hours rule — you prove it through coursework.

Can I mix DIY and listed courses?
Yes. Any combination works, as long as the 40 courses fit the major’s structure.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Forty out of forty, if you want. DIY is a way to prove a course, not a limited allowance.
