How Much Does NewU Cost?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

$79.99 a month — which you can pause at any time and pick up right where you left off — or $999 once. All of your course materials will only ever be free online things, and you will not incur tuition debt.

The monthly plan

$79.99 a month. You can pause it at any time, and pick up right where you left off whenever you’re ready. Progress is yours to pause indefinitely, as needed.

The one-time price

$999 once. At $79.99 a month, $999 costs about the same as 12 and a half months. So if you expect to finish in under about a year, the monthly plan costs less. If you expect to take longer — or you want to go at your own pace without watching the clock — the one-time price is cheaper.

What you get

Free online courses — from community colleges to Ivy League institutions and everything in between — organized into the 100 most common majors, or a major you design yourself. Coursework created from the course content, graded (by AI, with re-grading checks and a human backstop), and progress tracked with blockchain authentication. When you finish, a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education that any employer can verify for free, at any time.

What it is not

It is not free in the sense of costing nothing at all — the platform costs $79.99 a month or $999 once. And it is not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education.
