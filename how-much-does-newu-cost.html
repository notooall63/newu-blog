How Much Does NewU Cost?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

$79.99 a month or $999 once, per major. All of your course materials will only ever be free online things, and you will not incur tuition debt. You pay for a major before you enroll in it.

The monthly plan

$79.99 a month, per major. You can pause it at any time, and pick up right where you left off whenever you’re ready. Progress is yours to pause indefinitely, as needed.

The one-time price

$999 once, per major. At $79.99 a month, $999 costs about the same as 12 and a half months. So if you expect to finish in under about a year, the monthly plan costs less. If you expect to take longer — or you want to go at your own pace without watching the clock — the one-time price is cheaper.

Each major requires its own payment: a payment for one major cannot be transferred to another major, and there is no switching majors once a payment has been applied to it.

Single-course certificates: $19.99 each

You do not have to enroll in a major to earn a certificate. A Certificate of Completion for a single DIY Course costs $19.99, paid before you submit the coursework. It certifies that one course, and it is verifiable by any employer, the same as the major certificate.

Rollover: single-course certificates lower the one-time major price

A Certificate of Completion can roll over into a major, counting as one of its 40 courses. Each certificate rolled over takes $25 off the $999 one-time fee — or $12.49 if the course needed AI enhancement to meet the post-secondary standard — with the discounts adding up to a $0 floor. The discount applies to the one-time fee only: it never reduces the $79.99-a-month price. For a certificate that needed enhancement, the $12.49 comes off at enrollment, but it counts as one course only after you complete the enhanced course. A DIY Course completed inside a major counts as one of the 40 but takes no discount off, because you already paid for the major.

What you get

Free online courses — from community colleges to Ivy League institutions and everything in between — organized into the 100 most common majors, or a major you design yourself. Coursework created from the course content, graded (by AI, with re-grading checks and a human backstop), and progress tracked with blockchain authentication. When you finish, a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education that any employer can verify for free, at any time.

What it is not

It is not free in the sense of costing nothing at all — the platform costs $79.99 a month or $999 once, per major. And it is not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really pause and come back later?
Yes. The monthly plan can be paused at any time, and you pick up right where you left off whenever you’re ready.

Are the course materials really free?
Yes. All of your course materials will only ever be free online things. You will not incur tuition debt.

Can I pay for just one course instead of a whole major?
Yes. A single DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99, with no major enrollment and no monthly plan. If it later rolls into a major, it takes $25 off the $999 one-time fee ($12.49 if it was enhanced) — never off the monthly price. For a certificate that needed enhancement, the $12.49 comes off at enrollment, but it counts as one course only after you complete the enhanced course.

The bottom line

NewU costs $79.99 a month or $999 once, per major — plus $19.99 per single-course certificate if you want one. No tuition debt, no paid course materials.
