If I Cancel or Pause My Monthly Plan, Do I Lose My Progress or My Certificates?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

No. Your progress is anchored to Bitcoin and cannot be lost, your certificates stay valid and verifiable, and you keep read access to your records forever — even if you cancel or pause.

What pausing does

The monthly plan costs $79.99 a month, and you can pause it at any time. While it is paused, you are not charged, and you pick up right where you left off whenever you are ready. Pausing stops new graded work — you need an active plan to submit coursework — but it touches nothing you have already done. Your completed courses, your grades, and your certificates are all still there, exactly as they were.

What cancelling does

Cancelling is the same as pausing, except you are not coming back on the monthly plan. Your progress is blockchain-anchored, so it cannot be lost: every course you completed is a permanent public record on Bitcoin. Your certificates stay valid and verifiable, and an employer can still check them through the link you issued. You keep read access to your records forever — you can still view and download your transcript and your certificates. The only thing you lose is the ability to submit new graded coursework until you start a plan again.

Frequently asked questions

Can I come back later?
Yes. Restart the monthly plan whenever you are ready, and pick up right where you left off.

Does a pause affect my certificates?
No. Certificates stay valid and verifiable, and you keep read access to your records forever.

The bottom line

Cancel or pause whenever you need to. Your progress is anchored permanently, your certificates stay valid, and your records are yours forever.
