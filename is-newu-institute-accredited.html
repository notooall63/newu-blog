Is NewU Institute Accredited?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

No. NewU Institute is not an accredited university, and it does not award a degree. That is stated plainly, because it matters: if you need a degree from an accredited university, NewU Institute is not that, and it does not claim to be.

What NewU offers instead

A verifiable Certificate of Post-Secondary Education. NewU Institute turns free online courses — from community colleges to Ivy League institutions and everything in between — into real majors that are commensurate with accredited institutions’ majors, creates coursework from those courses, grades your work, and tracks your progress with blockchain authentication. When you finish, any employer can verify your Certificate for free, in seconds, at any time.

How the standard is kept

Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass, and 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. If your explanation comes up short, it goes to an actual human reviewer, and no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision. Your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it. NewU says it plainly on its homepage: “The rigors of this medium meet or exceed the traditional avenues in every respect that matters for a fulfilling and useful education.”

Who it is for — and who it is not for

It is for someone who wants a real, checkable education record without tuition debt and without going back to a traditional campus. It is not for someone who needs a degree from an accredited university — for example, where an employer, a licensing body, or a program requires one. The honest answer depends on what the credential has to do for you.

The bottom line

NewU Institute is not accredited, and it says so plainly. What it offers instead is a verifiable record of real work — graded, checked, and anchored to the blockchain — that an employer can confirm on their own.
