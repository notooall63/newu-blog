Is NewU Institute Open to Anyone?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes — with one exception. Anyone 13 or older can join NewU, from anywhere in the world. If you are under 18, you need a parent or guardian’s permission. There is no high school diploma requirement, no entrance test, no admission gate, and no government ID required.

No qualifications needed

There is no entrance test, no diploma requirement, and no admission gate. Anyone can enroll — in any major, at any time, or in a major they design themselves. See Do I need a high school diploma or any qualifications to join?.

No borders

Anyone, anywhere, online. There is no geographic restriction, and employer verification works worldwide. See Can I use NewU from outside the United States?.

No ID check

NewU never asks for a government ID, and never verifies your name, date of birth, or address against any record. The only two things that must be real are your email address and your photograph — the photograph is how future verifiers confirm the work is yours. See What Is NewU Institute’s Privacy Policy?.

The one exception: 13 and older, with a parent or guardian’s permission under 18

NewU is open to users 13 and older. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must give permission for you to use NewU. That is the only age rule — there is no upper age limit, and no requirement about where you went to school or what you studied before.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a high school diploma?
No. No diploma, no entrance test, no admission gate.

Can I join from outside the United States?
Yes. Anyone, anywhere, online.

Do I need to be a US citizen?
No. There is no citizenship requirement and no geographic restriction.

Is there an upper age limit?
No. The only age rule is 13 and older — and under 18s need a parent or guardian’s permission.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Open to anyone 13 or older, anywhere in the world — under 18s with a parent or guardian’s permission — no diploma, no test, no gate.
