Is There a Platform That Allows Me to Create My Own College Courses or College-Commensurate Courses?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. NewU Institute lets you create your own college-level courses — the platform calls them DIY Courses. You choose the topic, learn it anywhere you like, and prove what you learned on NewU through coursework and AI grading. A DIY Course can count toward a major, and a single one earns its own certificate.

How it works

You start with a topic you want to learn — something from your work, your reading, or a subject you have always wanted to study properly. You learn it your way: a book, a video, a free online course, or your own study. NewU never makes you log into another platform or buy course materials to do it.

Then you prove it. You submit coursework on the topic — a written case analysis, with citations to the sources you used — and NewU’s AI grades it. You need 70 out of 100 to pass, the same bar as every other NewU course. Random finished courses also get a spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words, and a human reviewer is the backstop.

What “college-commensurate” means here

A DIY Course counts toward a major only when it meets NewU’s post-secondary standard — the same level of depth and rigour as a college course. That is judged from your topic and your coursework, not from where you learned it. If your topic is real but too narrow on its own, it is not rejected: the AI builds it into a full post-secondary course, and you do the coursework on the enhanced topic. There is no cap: all 40 courses in a major can be DIY Courses if you want them to be.

And a DIY Course does not have to be part of a major at all. Taken on its own, it earns a Certificate of Completion ($19.99) — a verifiable record of that one course, anchored to Bitcoin, that any employer can check.

What it costs

If you are enrolled in a major, a DIY Course inside that major is part of your plan — $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once for lifetime access. A stand-alone DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99. And a Certificate of Completion can roll into a major later: one course, $25 off the $999 one-time fee — $12.49 if the certificate needed enhancement. The discount comes off the one-time fee only, never off the monthly plan.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be enrolled in a major to create a course?
No. You can take a single DIY Course on its own and earn a Certificate of Completion for it, without enrolling in a major.

Who decides whether my topic counts?
The AI checks whether your topic fits a major’s course type and meets the post-secondary standard. No person approves topics. And relevance is only judged when you ask to roll a certificate into a specific major — never promised in advance.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Yes — there is a platform for it, and it is NewU Institute. Pick a topic, learn it anywhere, prove it through graded coursework, and count it toward a major or earn a certificate for that one course alone.
