Is There a Way to Get a Certificate for Self-Study?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Yes. On NewU Institute, a DIY Course turns self-study into a Certificate of Completion ($19.99). You learn the topic anywhere — a book, a video, a free online course, your own study — then prove what you learned through coursework and AI grading. When you pass, you get a certificate anchored to Bitcoin that any employer can verify in seconds.

How it works

You propose the topic you studied, then submit coursework on it: a written case analysis, with citations to the sources you used. NewU’s AI grades it, and you need 70 out of 100 to pass — the same bar as every other NewU course. You have up to 3 submissions for each assignment, and your highest grade stands. Random finished courses also get a spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words, and a human reviewer is the backstop.

There is no deadline, no hours requirement for a single DIY Course, and no requirement to have studied recently. You prove what you know now. And you do not have to enroll in a major: a stand-alone DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99, with no monthly plan required.

What the certificate proves

The Certificate of Completion is a record of one course: what you studied, when, and how it was graded. It is anchored to Bitcoin, so the record is permanent and cannot be edited, deleted, or lost. An employer checks it through a verification link you issue — they see the credential, your name, your photograph, and a VALID verdict, backed by the Bitcoin anchor. Your photograph is never printed on the certificate itself; it only appears on the verification page, so the employer can confirm the work is yours.

The certificate can also roll into a major later. One Certificate of Completion counts as one course under one major, and takes $25 off the $999 one-time fee ($12.49 if it needed enhancement), off the one-time fee only. It stays valid on its own even after it rolls over.

What it is not

A Certificate of Completion is not a degree, and NewU Institute is not an accredited university — the certificate carries no accreditation, and it is not transfer credit at an accredited college. What it is, is proof of completed, graded work on a topic you chose yourself: evidence an employer can verify themselves, for free, in seconds, instead of taking your word for it.
