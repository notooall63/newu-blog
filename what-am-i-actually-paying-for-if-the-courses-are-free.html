What Am I Actually Paying for If the Courses Are Free?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

The course materials are free — that part is real. What you pay for is everything NewU does around them: the coursework, the AI grading, the progress tracking, the blockchain verification, and the certificate. Free content on its own gives you knowledge; NewU turns it into a record an employer can check.

What the price covers

NewU organizes free online courses into structured majors — 100 of them, each 40 courses across Major Core, General Education, and Free Electives — or a major you design yourself. For every course, NewU creates the coursework, grades it with AI (70 out of 100 to pass), re-checks the grading for consistency, and tracks your progress toward the major. When you finish a course, the result is anchored to Bitcoin, so any employer can independently verify what you studied, when, and how it was graded. And when you finish the major, you get a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education. None of that comes free with a free course.

What the price does not cover

It does not buy you the course materials — those are free online things, and you will not incur tuition debt for them. It does not buy you a degree: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. And it does not buy you a pass — you still do the coursework, and you still have to pass it. The price is $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once, and a single DIY Course with its own Certificate of Completion is $19.99.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the free courses without paying anything?
Yes. You can browse the catalog and the free course content without a plan. You only pay when you want graded coursework and certificates.

Why not just watch free courses on my own?
You can — and many people do. But on your own you have no structure, no grading, and no proof. NewU adds those three things, and the proof is the part an employer can check.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

You are not paying for course materials — those are free. You are paying for coursework, grading, tracking, verification, and a certificate: the things that turn free learning into a record anyone can check.
