What Changes on NewU When I Turn 18?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Almost nothing. Your progress, your certificates, and your plan all carry over unchanged. Two things change: the guardian permission no longer applies to you, and you are offered a one-time new verification photograph.

The new photograph

If your photograph was confirmed while you were under 18, you are offered a new one when you turn 18, so employers see a current photo of you. The offer appears as a prompt after you sign in. It is not a block: you can dismiss it and keep using NewU, and it shows again on later sign-ins until you take the new photo. Your old photograph stays in place until you replace it. After the new photograph is confirmed, the 15-year rule applies from the new confirmation date. See Why does NewU need my photo, and where does it appear?.

The 15-year rule

Your photograph is locked once it is confirmed, and you can update it once every 15 years. The update at 18 is a one-time extra on top of that rule — it does not use up your 15-year update.

The guardian permission

If you signed up at 13 to 17 with a parent or guardian’s permission, that permission covered your sign-up. Once you are 18, the permission requirement no longer applies to you. See What Should a Parent or Guardian Know About NewU?.

What does not change

Nothing resets. Your courses, your progress, your certificates, your plan, and your verification links all stay exactly as they were. Your certificates stay valid and verifiable.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to take the new photograph?
No. It is offered, not required. You can dismiss the prompt, but it will show again until you take the new photo.

What happens to my old photograph?
It stays in place until you replace it.

Does anything happen to my certificates?
No. They stay valid and verifiable, and nothing about them changes.

What if I signed up at 18 or older?
Nothing changes for you. The update at 18 only applies if your photograph was confirmed while you were under 18.

Can I update my photograph at any other time?
Once every 15 years. Your photograph is locked after it is confirmed, apart from the 15-year update and the one-time update at 18.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — sign in and the prompt will appear if the update applies to you. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Turning 18 changes almost nothing on NewU: your work carries over, the guardian permission no longer applies, and you are offered a one-time new verification photo.
