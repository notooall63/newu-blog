What Does the Coursework Actually Look Like?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

It depends on the course. For a course in a major, it is a 1,000–1,500-word case analysis with citations, graded by AI at 70 out of 100 to pass. For a stand-alone DIY course, the assignment is tailored to the topic — a written analysis for a knowledge topic, the work itself for a visual skill, or written questions and answers in the language for a language topic. Either way, it is real work, not a multiple-choice quiz.

What you actually write

For a course in a major, NewU’s AI generates an assignment built from the course content — usually a case analysis set in a real-world situation, with a rubric that spells out what good work looks like. You write your analysis in your own words, citing the sources you used, and submit it on the platform. For a stand-alone DIY course, the AI creates the assignment and the rubric for that particular topic instead. There is no exam hall, no timer, and no due date: it is self-paced, and you submit when you are ready.

How it is graded

Your coursework is graded by AI against the rubric, and you need 70 out of 100 to pass — the same pass mark for every course. But the assignment and the rubric are not the same for every course: a course in a major uses the post-secondary case analysis and rubric, while a stand-alone DIY course gets an assignment and a rubric tailored to its topic. The grading is re-checked for consistency, and anything flagged gets a human review. You have up to 3 submissions per coursework, and your highest grade stands, so a weak first attempt does not sink you. Random finished courses also get a spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words, and a human reviewer is the backstop.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to write in a particular style?
It depends on the course. For a course in a major, you write a case analysis in your own words, with citations to the sources you used. For a stand-alone DIY course, the assignment is tailored to the topic. Either way, the rubric tells you what the grader is looking for.

What if I fail?
You get up to 3 submissions per coursework, your highest grade stands, and you can retake a course as many times as you like. Your plan covers the grading — there is no extra charge.

The bottom line

Coursework on NewU is real work, not a quiz: a case analysis for a course in a major, or work tailored to the topic for a stand-alone DIY course — graded by AI at 70 to pass, with retakes if you need them.
