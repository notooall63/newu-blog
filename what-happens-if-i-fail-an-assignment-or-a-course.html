What Happens If I Fail an Assignment or a Course?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Nothing bad. You get up to 3 submissions per coursework, your highest grade stands, and you can retake a course as many times as you like — at no extra charge. Your plan covers the grading.

How failing works on NewU

Coursework is graded by AI at 70 out of 100 to pass. If your first submission comes in under 70, it is not the end of the story: you can submit again, up to 3 times for that coursework, and only your highest grade counts. So a weak first attempt does not sink you — you read the feedback, improve the work, and try again. If you still do not pass after 3 submissions, you can retake the whole course, as many times as you like. There is no penalty for retaking, and no extra charge: grading is covered by your plan, $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once.

What failing does not do

Failing does not lock you out, does not cost you progress you already earned, and does not go on any permanent record an employer sees — employers see only your completed courses and certificates, which are anchored to Bitcoin. It also does not trigger any action against your account. The only thing that ever involves a human reviewer is a spot check — and even there, no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision.

Frequently asked questions

Do retakes cost extra?
No. Your plan covers grading, including retakes. There is no per-attempt charge.

Can I see why I failed?
Yes. You get feedback on your submission, so you know what to improve before you submit again.

Where do I start?

The bottom line

Failing on NewU costs you nothing but another try: 3 submissions per coursework, highest grade stands, unlimited retakes, no extra charge.
