What Happens If My DIY Course Topic Is Too Narrow?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

It does not get rejected. If your topic is related to a major but too narrow on its own to be a post-secondary course, NewU’s AI builds it into a full post-secondary course. You do the coursework on the enhanced topic, and it counts toward your major.

How the enhancement works

Say you learned how to draw one cartoon character. That is a real skill, and it is related to art — but on its own it is a sliver of a subject, not a course. The AI takes that topic and defines a broader course that builds on it — for example, character design and visual storytelling: proportion, expression, how a character reads on the page, why the design works.

You see your original topic next to the enhanced course, with a plain explanation of what changed and why, and you have to accept the enhanced course before doing any coursework on it. Nothing is forced: if you do not accept, your original topic stays as it is, and it simply does not count toward the major. If you do accept, you complete coursework on the enhanced topic, graded at the same 70-out-of-100 bar. The enhanced course has to meet the same post-secondary standard as any other course — it is not just a renamed version of the thin topic.

When enhancement does not apply

Enhancement is only for topics that are related to a major but too narrow. A topic that is not related to any part of the major at all cannot be rolled over into it, and cannot be enhanced into something related. That still cannot count toward the major — though a stand-alone certificate for it stays valid on its own.

What happens to the original certificate

If you earned a Certificate of Completion for the thin topic first and then rolled it over with an enhancement, the original certificate stays valid as its own certificate. The major credit is for the enhanced course. The rollover discount is smaller for an enhanced certificate — $12.49 off the $999 one-time fee, instead of $25 for a certificate that met the standard as-is — and the discount applies to the one-time fee only, never to the $79.99-a-month price.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to accept the enhanced course?
No. You see the original topic and the enhanced course side by side, and you choose. If you decline, nothing changes.

Does enhancement mean I did something wrong?
No. It means the topic was a good start that needed more breadth to be a full course. The AI’s job is to build it up, not to punish you for starting narrow.

The bottom line

A narrow topic is a starting point, not a dead end. NewU’s AI helps you build it into a full post-secondary course — you still do the work, and you still earn the credit.
