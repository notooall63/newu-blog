What Happens to My Certificate If NewU Ever Closes?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

Your certificate is anchored to Bitcoin — a permanent public record that does not depend on NewU staying in business. The anchor outlives the platform.

How the anchor works

Every completed course on NewU is anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain through OpenTimestamps. Only hashes are anchored — never your photograph or personal details — so the on-chain record does not identify you, but it does prove exactly what was studied, when, and how it was graded. A blockchain record is permanent: it cannot be edited or deleted by you, by NewU, or by anyone else. That is the feature that makes your credentials verifiable, and it is also what protects them if NewU ever closes. The anchor is a public record on Bitcoin, not a file on NewU’s servers.

What you hold yourself

You also hold your own copies: your certificate is a signed PDF you can download and keep, with a QR code linking to the verification record, and your transcript is a verified, printable record of your courses and progress — a transcript page plus a downloadable PDF — free to view and download forever. Viewing and copying an existing certificate is always free. So even in the worst case, you keep the certificate, the transcript, and the Bitcoin anchor that backs them.

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone delete my anchor?
No. On-chain records are permanent and cannot be edited or deleted — that is the point of anchoring to Bitcoin.

Does the anchor identify me?
No. Only hashes are anchored, never your photograph or personal details. The on-chain record does not identify you.

The bottom line

If NewU ever closes, your certificate does not go with it. The Bitcoin anchor is permanent, and your own copies are yours to keep.
