What If My Credential Link Does Not Work?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

If you saved a credential link a while ago, it still works — it takes you to the current credential. If a link shows “Credential unavailable,” the address is not recognized: open My Learning and use the current link there.

“Credential unavailable” — what it means

That page means the credential address you opened does not match a credential. Check the address for typos, or go to My Learning and open the credential from there — the link there is always current.

If you are signed out

Opening your own credential link while signed out asks you to sign in first. That is expected — sign in and the credential opens.

If an employer says your link does not work

An employer opening a verification link without a valid grant sees “Not verified.” Share a fresh verification link from your credential page — the employer needs no account, just the current link. See How can an employer verify what I learned on my own? for the full picture.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be signed in to view my own credential?
Yes. Your own credential page asks you to sign in first; employer verification links work without an account when they carry a valid grant.

Can an employer check my credential without an account?
Yes — with a valid verification link, no account is needed.

The bottom line

Your credential lives at one current address, and My Learning always has it.
