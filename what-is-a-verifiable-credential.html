What Is a Verifiable Credential?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

A verifiable credential is a record of work that someone else can check without taking your word for it — or the platform’s word for it. On NewU Institute, that record is the Certificate of Post-Secondary Education, backed by progress anchored to the blockchain step by step. Any employer can check it themselves in seconds, for free, at any time.

What makes it verifiable

The record is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it — no changed grades, no fudged passes, nothing. The Certificate’s courses and work mean YOU did the work, YOU put in the time, YOU know the materials. An employer doesn’t have to call the school or trust a PDF — they can check the record themselves.

What it proves

That you completed a major — one of 100 curated structures across nine fields, or a major you designed yourself — that your coursework was graded, and that your progress was checked along the way. Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass, and 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. If your explanation comes up short, it goes to an actual human reviewer, and no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision.

What it is not

It is not a degree from an accredited university. NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education. The credential is verifiable — that is the point — but it is not a degree, and NewU does not claim it is.

Frequently asked questions

Who can verify it?
Any employer, in seconds, for free, at any time.

Can the record be changed after the fact?
No. Your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it.

Do I need a government ID to sign up?
No. Your identity, your avatar — no government ID required. Only your email address and your photo must be real. Your photo is only ever used to verify that the work is yours.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

A verifiable credential is proof you can show, not a claim you have to make. That is the whole idea: real work, graded and recorded, that someone else can check in seconds.
