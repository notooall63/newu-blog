What Is an Enhanced Course?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

An enhanced course is a full post-secondary course that NewU’s AI builds from a DIY topic that is real but too narrow on its own to count toward a major. Your topic is not rejected — it is widened into a course that meets NewU’s post-secondary standard, and you do the coursework on the enhanced topic.

How it works

Say you propose the DIY topic “How to Balance a Checkbook.” It is a real skill, and it fits an Accounting major, but on its own it is too narrow to count as a full college-level course. Instead of turning it down, the AI builds it into a broader course — for example, “Cash Management, Reconciliation, and Internal Controls.” You see the original topic next to the enhanced course, with a plain explanation of what changed and why, and you must accept the enhanced course before doing any coursework. The coursework is done on the enhanced topic, at the same 70-out-of-100 bar as every other NewU course.

What happens to your original certificate

Nothing bad. If you already earned a Certificate of Completion for the original topic on its own, that certificate stays valid — it is still yours, still verifiable, and still proves what you studied. Enhancement does not take it away. What changes is what the certificate can do inside a major: at rollover, a certificate that needed enhancement is “partially applicable,” and it is offered the enhanced course. If you accept and complete the enhanced coursework, it rolls over at $12.49 off the $999 one-time fee — half the usual $25 — because the enhanced course is what brings it up to the standard. The discount comes off the one-time fee only, never off the monthly price.

What enhancement is not

Enhancement is only for topics that are related to a major but too thin. A topic that has nothing to do with any course type cannot be enhanced into relevance — the AI cannot turn an unrelated topic into a course that fits. And enhancement never changes the standard: the enhanced course has to meet NewU’s post-secondary standard in full before it counts toward a major. It is also not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education for a full major, or a Certificate of Completion for a single course.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay extra for an enhanced course?
No. There is no enhancement fee. Inside a major, the enhanced course is part of your plan — $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once.

Can I say no to the enhanced course?
Yes. You see the original and the enhanced version before anything happens, and nothing moves forward until you accept it. If you do not accept it, you keep your original certificate as a stand-alone credential.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

An enhanced course is NewU’s way of saying yes to a real topic that is just too narrow: the AI builds it into a full post-secondary course, you accept it, and you do the work on the enhanced topic — and your original certificate stays valid either way.
