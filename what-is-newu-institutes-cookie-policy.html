What Is NewU Institute’s Cookie Policy?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

NewU does not use cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. Your sign-in session lives in your browser’s local storage, not in a cookie — and NewU runs no advertising trackers.

How sign-in works without cookies

NewU’s privacy policy puts it in one line: “We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in; we do not use cookies.” Local storage is a small store in your own browser that keeps you signed in. Unlike a cookie, it is not sent to the server with every request — it stays on your device. If you tick “Keep Me Signed In For 30 Days” when you sign in, the session lasts 30 days; otherwise it ends when you close your browser. If you clear your browser’s stored data for NewU, you will need to sign in again with a fresh code.

What NewU does not run

No advertising trackers. No analytics profiles sold to anyone. No collecting your work for advertising. The only technical data NewU keeps is standard server logs — IP address and browser type — for security and operation. See What Is NewU Institute’s Privacy Policy? for the full picture, and the policy itself at sites.google.com/view/newuprivacypolicy/home.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no cookie banner?
Because there are no cookies. A banner would be asking permission for something NewU does not do.

Does NewU track me?
No advertising trackers, no analytics profiles. Standard server logs only, for security and operation.

What is local storage?
A small store in your own browser. NewU uses it to keep you signed in; it stays on your device and is not sent to the server with every request the way a cookie is.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

No cookies, no trackers, no banner — your sign-in lives in your own browser, and that is the whole of it.
