What Is NewU Institute’s Privacy Policy?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

NewU collects as little as possible, and never checks who you are. No government ID is required. The only two things that must be real are your email address and your photograph. You decide who sees your work, and nothing about your coursework, grades, or certificates is shared with anyone by default. This post summarizes the policy; the full policy is the official version, effective November 19, 2026, at sites.google.com/view/newuprivacypolicy/home. NewU is operated by Amanda’s Awards Co., a New Jersey S-Corporation.

What NewU collects

Your email address, verified when you sign up, and the profile details you choose to enter — name, date of birth, address, phone number, emergency contact, and highest education. None of those are verified against any ID or database. A recovery email, verified during setup, used only if you lose access to your account. A verification photograph, which is stored and shown back to you on your account page; it is used only to verify that the work is yours, is never shown on your certificate, and is never used for anything else — NewU does not run face matching or any biometric identification on it. Once confirmed, your photograph is locked; one optional update becomes available every 15 years. NewU also keeps your coursework and grades, your DIY Course proposals, your certificate records, and the email addresses of people you send verification links to — used only to deliver the link. Payments are processed by Square; NewU does not see or store your full card number. The only technical data is standard server logs — IP address and browser type — for security and operation.

What NewU does not collect — and does not do

No government ID, and your name, address, or date of birth are never verified against any record. No advertising trackers, no analytics profiles sold, and your work is never collected for advertising. NewU does not use cookies at all — see What Is NewU Institute’s Cookie Policy? — and your sign-in session lives in your browser’s local storage instead.

Who sees your work

You do. Nobody else, by default. You may share your coursework, grades, and certificates with people you choose, and you can take that access away at any time. NewU reviewers and administrators may view your coursework and grades when carrying out grading checks, spot checks, or fraud review — that is part of operating the platform, not sharing.

Where your data goes

Only to the services that run the platform: Square for payments, Cloudflare Workers AI for grading, tutoring, coursework generation, and DIY enhancement and rollover assessment — Cloudflare does not keep your content after the request is done and does not use it to train AI models — Serper for live course searches, which sees only the search words, Resend for email delivery, Supabase for account and coursework storage, Filebase for course file storage, and the blockchain networks, which receive credential hashes only. NewU does not sell your data, and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Law enforcement receives data only when legally compelled.

The blockchain part — read this before you finish a course

Course completions and certificates are anchored to a public blockchain. On-chain data — credential hashes and timestamps — is public and permanent: it cannot be edited or deleted by you or by NewU. Because only hashes are anchored, never your photograph or personal details, the on-chain record does not identify you. See What happens to my certificate if NewU ever closes?.

Your control

Verification grants are issued by you, to an email address you provide, as a link. Anyone with the link can view the credential, and a grant stays valid until you revoke it. If your account is suspended, your grants stop working until your account is restored. To request deletion of your off-chain data — account, coursework records, and your verification photograph — email newuinstitute@aol.com. Your photograph is deleted when your whole account is deleted; it cannot be deleted on its own while your account remains, because it is what ties your coursework to you. On-chain anchors cannot be deleted. Backups may retain data for a limited period before expiring.

Sign-in, and who NewU is for

You sign in with your email address and a one-time code sent to that email. If you choose “Keep Me Signed In For 30 Days,” your session lasts 30 days; otherwise it ends when you close your browser. There is no password to forget and no seed phrase to lose. NewU is open to users 13 and older. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must give permission for you to use NewU. Material changes to the policy are announced on the platform.

Frequently asked questions

Does NewU sell my data?
No. NewU does not sell your data, and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Is my coursework used to train AI?
No. Your work is never used to train AI models, and Cloudflare does not keep your content after grading is done.

Can I delete my account?
You can request deletion of your off-chain data by emailing newuinstitute@aol.com. On-chain anchors are permanent and cannot be deleted.

Who can see my grades?
Only people you share them with — plus reviewers and administrators carrying out grading checks, spot checks, or fraud review.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

As little collected as possible, nothing sold, nothing shared by default — and the full policy, not this summary, is the official version.
