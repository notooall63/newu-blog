What Is the Cheapest Route to a Post-Secondary Education?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

The cheapest route is one where the learning material costs nothing and you only pay for the structure, the grading, and the proof. That is NewU Institute’s model: all of your course materials will only ever be free online things, and you will not incur tuition debt. The platform itself costs $79.99 a month — which you can pause at any time and pick up right where you left off — or $999 once.

The two prices, compared

$79.99 a month or $999 once. At $79.99 a month, the $999 one-time price costs about the same as 12 and a half months. So if you expect to finish in under about a year, the monthly plan costs less. If you expect to take longer — or you want to go at your own pace without watching the clock — the one-time price is cheaper. And because the monthly plan can be paused at any time, you can stop and pick up right where you left off whenever you’re ready.

What you get for it

Free online courses — from community colleges to Ivy League institutions and everything in between — organized into the 100 most common majors, or a major you design yourself. Coursework created from the course content, graded (by AI, with re-grading checks and a human backstop), and progress tracked with blockchain authentication. When you finish, a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education that any employer can verify for free, at any time. Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass.

What it is not

It is not free in the sense of costing nothing at all — the platform costs $79.99 a month or $999 once. And it is not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. What you earn is a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education.

The bottom line

The cheapest route to a post-secondary education is free materials plus a low, flat platform cost and no tuition debt — $79.99 a month if you’ll move fast, $999 once if you’ll take your time.
