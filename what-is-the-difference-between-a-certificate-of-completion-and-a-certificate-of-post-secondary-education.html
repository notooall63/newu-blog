What Is the Difference Between a Certificate of Completion and a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

A Certificate of Completion certifies one course. A Certificate of Post-Secondary Education certifies a full major — all 40 courses. Both are real, verifiable NewU credentials, and one can lead to the other: a Certificate of Completion can roll over and count as one course toward a major.

Certificate of Completion: one course

This is the certificate for a single DIY Course. You learn a subject anywhere, submit coursework on it, and pass at 70 out of 100. It costs $19.99, and you do not need to enroll in a major or hold a monthly plan to earn one. The certificate carries the name of the course you completed. It is anchored to the blockchain and carries a QR code and an employer verification link, so anyone can check it in seconds, for free.

Certificate of Post-Secondary Education: a full major

This is the certificate for completing an entire major: 40 courses — core, general education, and electives — each passed at 70 out of 100, with coursework graded and progress anchored to the blockchain step by step. It comes with a transcript, and the same kind of employer verification: a link and QR code anyone can check, for free, at any time. You earn it by enrolling in a major, either $79.99 a month (pausable at any time) or $999 once.

How they relate

The two certificates are not in competition. A Certificate of Completion can roll over into a major, counting as one of its 40 courses, and each one rolled over takes $25 off the $999 one-time fee — or $12.49 if the course needed AI enhancement to meet the post-secondary standard. The discount applies to the one-time fee only, never to the monthly price. The Certificate of Completion stays valid on its own after it rolls over. And a DIY Course completed inside a major counts as one of the 40, but takes no discount off the price — you already paid for the major.

What neither one is

Neither certificate is a degree, and NewU Institute is not an accredited university. A Certificate of Completion certifies one course; a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education certifies a full major. Both say exactly what they are, and both can be verified by anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Which one should I start with?
If you want to prove one subject, start with a Certificate of Completion. If you want the full major credential, enroll in a major — your single-course certificates can roll in as you go.

Can an employer tell them apart?
Yes. Each certificate states what it is — the course name for a Certificate of Completion, the major for a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education — and the verification page shows it plainly.
