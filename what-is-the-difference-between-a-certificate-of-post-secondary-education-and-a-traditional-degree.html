What Is the Difference Between a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education and a Traditional Degree?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

A traditional degree is awarded by an accredited university. A Certificate of Post-Secondary Education from NewU Institute is awarded after you complete a major built from free online courses, with graded coursework and progress tracked with blockchain authentication. NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree. The difference is the issuer and the credential — not necessarily the rigor of the work behind it.

What a traditional degree is

A traditional degree is a credential awarded by an accredited university when you complete its program. The university is the issuer, and the degree is the credential. That is the model NewU Institute is an alternative to — it describes itself as “The Original Verifiable Alternative Post-Secondary Education Platform.”

What NewU’s Certificate is

NewU Institute turns free online courses — from community colleges to Ivy League institutions and everything in between — into real majors, creates coursework from those courses, grades your work, and tracks your progress with blockchain authentication. When you finish a major, it issues a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education, which any potential employer may freely verify at any time.

The Certificate is based on step-by-step progress verified on the blockchain, so any employer can check it themselves in seconds, for free, at any time. Your progress is anchored to the blockchain step by step, so neither the application nor its administrators can alter it — no changed grades, no fudged passes, nothing.

The key differences

Who issues it. A traditional degree is issued by an accredited university. NewU’s Certificate is issued by NewU Institute, which is not an accredited university.

What it is called. A degree versus a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education. The names are different because the credentials are different — and NewU says so plainly rather than calling its Certificate a degree.

How it is verified. A NewU Certificate is verifiable on the blockchain, so any employer can check it themselves in seconds, for free, at any time. The record is the proof: the courses and the work behind it are yours, and the record of it cannot be rewritten after the fact.

What it costs. A NewU major carries no tuition debt: all of your course materials will only ever be free online things, and the platform itself costs $79.99 a month — which you can pause at any time and pick up right where you left off — or $999 once.

How it is earned. You learn at your own pace through your chosen major’s free courses, complete its AI-generated coursework, get it graded, and build a verified record one step at a time — which you are free to pause indefinitely, as needed.

What stays the same

The rigor. NewU Institute says it plainly on its homepage: “The rigors of this medium meet or exceed the traditional avenues in every respect that matters for a fulfilling and useful education.” Every piece of coursework and every course requires 70 out of 100 to pass, and 1 in 4 finished courses gets a random spot check asking you to explain a piece of your own work in your own words. If your explanation comes up short, it goes to an actual human reviewer, and no action is ever taken without a human-reviewed decision.

That is also why the certificate means something to an employer: the courses and the work behind it are yours. You did the work, you put in the time, you know the materials.

Which one do you need?

If a degree from an accredited university is what you need — because an employer, a licensing body, or a program requires one — NewU Institute is not that, and it does not claim to be. If what you need is a real, checkable record of post-secondary work without tuition debt, the Certificate is built for that. The honest answer depends on what the credential has to do for you.
