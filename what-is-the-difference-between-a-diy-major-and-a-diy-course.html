What Is the Difference Between a DIY Major and a DIY Course?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

A DIY major is a whole 40-course major you design. A DIY course is a single course you design — learn a topic anywhere, prove it on NewU. One is the program; the other is one course inside it.

The DIY major: the whole program

A DIY major is for when the major you want does not exist in the catalog of 100. You name it, and the AI matches it to the closest existing major and shows you that major’s structure — or you set your own structure, as long as it adds up to exactly 40 courses across Major Core, General Education, and Free Electives. You are the one who decides whether the structure is right for you. Once you save it, you can enroll in it right away, and you can submit it for the public catalog, where a reviewer can approve it for everyone.

The DIY course: one course

A DIY course is for when you want to learn one topic your way. You propose the topic, learn it anywhere — a book, a video, a free online course, your own study — and prove it on NewU through coursework and AI grading. It can count as one of the 40 courses in a major, as long as the topic fits that part of the major and the course meets NewU’s post-secondary standard. Or you can take it on its own, without enrolling in a major, and earn a Certificate of Completion for that single course ($19.99). There is no cap: all 40 courses in a major can be DIY Courses if you want them to be.

How they fit together

You can use both at once: design a DIY major, then fill it with DIY Courses you design yourself. Or you can take DIY Courses inside a catalog major, or on their own. The certificate is what differs: a DIY major earns a Certificate of Post-Secondary Education when you finish all 40 courses; a single DIY Course earns a Certificate of Completion. And it is still not a degree from an accredited university: NewU Institute is not an accredited university and does not award a degree.

Frequently asked questions

Can a DIY major use listed courses too?
Yes. A DIY major is your structure — you fill it with listed courses, DIY Courses, or both.

Can a DIY course count toward a DIY major?
Yes, as long as the topic fits one of the major’s course types and the course meets NewU’s post-secondary standard.

Where do I start?
At newu.institute — explore the 100 majors, or design your own. Questions? Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

DIY major: the whole 40-course program, designed by you. DIY course: one course, designed by you. Use either — or both.
