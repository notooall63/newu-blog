What Should a Parent or Guardian Know About NewU?

By NewU Institute

The short answer

If your child is 13 to 17, they can use NewU with your permission. When they sign up, NewU emails you to let you know. This page explains what that email means, what NewU does with your child’s information, and what you can do about it.

The email you receive

When a learner aged 13 to 17 signs up, NewU sends an email to the parent or guardian’s address. It says your child signed up for NewU, explains what NewU is, and explains that your child’s verification photograph is stored and shown to verifiers. If you did not approve the sign-up, or if you want the account deleted, reply to newuinstitute@aol.com.

The permission

At sign-up, the learner confirms that a parent or guardian has given them permission to use NewU, and gives the guardian’s name and email address. Once the account is created, it works normally — there is no restricted or waiting state.

The photograph

NewU asks every learner for a verification photograph, and the photograph has one job: confirming that the work is your child’s. It is shown to verifiers on the verification page, next to your child’s name — it is never on the certificate. It is deleted if the whole account is deleted. See Why does NewU need my photo, and where does it appear?.

Payments

Paid features for learners under 18 need a parent or guardian’s permission, and the guardian’s payment method is what pays. NewU costs $79.99 a month or $999 once, and a single-course certificate is $19.99. See How much does NewU cost?.

What you can do

You can contact NewU about your child’s account at newuinstitute@aol.com at any time, and you can ask for the account to be deleted. See What Is NewU Institute’s Privacy Policy? for what NewU collects and how it is used.

When your child turns 18

When a learner whose photograph was confirmed while they were under 18 turns 18, they are offered a one-time new photograph, so employers see a current photo. See What Changes on NewU When I Turn 18?.

Frequently asked questions

I got an email saying my child signed up for NewU. Is it real?
Yes, if it came from NewU after your child signed up. The email says your child signed up, explains what NewU is, and tells you how to object or ask for the account to be deleted.

Can I stop my child from using NewU?
Yes. Reply to newuinstitute@aol.com to object or to ask for the account to be deleted.

Does my child have to be 18 to use NewU?
No. Learners must be at least 13 years old, and learners under 18 need a parent or guardian’s permission. Learners under 13 cannot join.

Will my child’s photograph appear on their certificate?
No. The photograph is never on the certificate. It is shown only to verifiers on the verification page.

What does it cost?
$79.99 a month or $999 once for a major, and $19.99 for a single-course certificate. Your payment method is what pays for a learner under 18.

Where do I start if I have a question?
Email newuinstitute@aol.com.

The bottom line

Your child can use NewU from age 13 with your permission. You get an email when they sign up, you can object or ask for the account to be deleted at any time, and the photograph is used only to verify that the work is theirs.
